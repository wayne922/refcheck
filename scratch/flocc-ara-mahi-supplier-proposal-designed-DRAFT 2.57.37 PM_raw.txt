Ara Mahi
An employment activation service for ACC vocational
rehabilitation suppliers, delivered by FLOCC Career Pathways.
Ara Mahi improves participant readiness, employer engagement, and pathway
momentum for ACC vocational rehabilitation clients. It sits alongside ACC-funded
services including Back to Work, Pathways to Employment, Obtain Work Trial, and
Job Search — as a defined, subcontractable activation module.1
WHEN TO USE
Use Ara Mahi when the vocational direction is known, but employer engagement, market testing, or
participant readiness is slowing progress.

ARA MAHI · EXAMPLE PATHWAYS
HOW IT WORKS IN PRACTICE
Example participant pathways
Three illustrative pathways across different ACC service contexts.
Back to Work participant
A participant cannot return to a heavy manual role and is exploring possible light logistics,
customer service, or dispatch directions. FLOCC prepares the participant, scans local
employers, approaches up to 5 suitable employers, gathers role information, and hands viable
options back to the VOC supplier for clinical and vocational suitability review.
Pathway to Employment participant
A participant with complex needs, lower confidence, or an unclear work direction. FLOCC may
identify that direct employer contact is premature, focuses on readiness work and a targeted
market scan, and reports barriers, evidence, and staged next steps to support the supplier's
case planning.
Job Search participant
A participant who is vocationally independent but inactive in the labour market. FLOCC reviews
the CV and employment profile, identifies target roles, supports applications and employer
approaches, and records activity, employer responses, and any barriers encountered.
ONE PRACTICAL QUESTION
"Is there a real, suitable employer pathway for this participant, and if
so, what needs to happen next?"

ADD-ONS · KPIs
Optional add-ons

Out-of-component work is not started automatically. FLOCC will first consult with the VOC provider, confirm

MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK
Sample pilot KPIs
01 100% of accepted referrals receive activation review
02 First participant contact attempted within agreed timeframe
03 Up to 5 targeted employer approaches per participant within base scope
04 Supplier progress update completed for each participant
05 Barriers, activity, opportunities, and recommended next steps documented for each participant
06 Work trials, interviews, job offers, and placements documented when they occur (see outcomes
note)
NOTE ON OUTCOME MEASURES
Interviews, work trials, job offers, and placements are reported where achieved, not guaranteed
contractual deliverables. They depend on participant suitability, medical capacity, employer demand,
ACC approvals, and labour-market conditions.
