Tuakana-Teina (TT)
Our Careers Pathway Programme is Tuakana-Teina. It is designed to help our teens and
young adults prepare for life in the workforce. Our target demographic is young adults
between the ages of 18 and 24. TT is a 12 week programme with three pillars;
1. Health wellbeing and fitness
2. Work readiness
3. Practical work experience
This has enabled us to quickly become a credible and trusted partner to the sector that we support and we plan to continue in this
	way by supporting our clients in the Professional sector across New Zealand. In a Simple, Honest and Direct way!
All three pillars cover the full range of readiness for a young Maori entering adult life. The health, wellbeing and fitness
programme gives every individual access to a wide range of training programmes to improve their health and fitness with goals.
This 2 year membership feeds into the strong body, strong mind concept and is also the baseline foundation of our aftercare
programme.
The work readiness programme runs for 10 weeks. It fully prepares each individual with the skills and resources to find and attain
a job and be confident joining the workforce. Each individual can attend 30 group sessions and biweekly one on one link ups.
The practical work experience programme Through our pre-selection process, a student can complete a trial in the career
pathway they choose from either office, retail or trades.

programme and objectives
● We run 12 week cohorts with a key focus around life and work readiness
● Each cohort has 10 -15 participants between the ages of 18 and 25
The programme will cover the following:
Health and wellbeing
- 3 month fitness module - continues post course completion
- Health and Nutrition

Work readiness and experience

- Building confidence and value in the workplace

- To educate participants on healthy and productive life choices
- To give participants the tools to lead an active and healthy life
- To give participants the tools and resources to find a job
- To give participants the tools to feel confident in the workplace and start a new career
- To reduce the number of rangatahi on the benefit

Urban Athletes
Urban Athletes is your small, friendly, local community gym in Plimmerton, Porirua.
We Specialise in High Intensity Interval Training (HIIT), BoxFit and Strength group fitness
classes, personal training and regular fitness challenges. We have been operating since
2013 and have built a diverse community of ethnicities, background and ages; welcoming
everyone into the whānau. We built this business on the foundation of wanting to do good
things for our community.
This has enabled us to quickly become a credible and trusted partner to the sector that we support and we plan to continue in this
	way by supporting our clients in the Professional sector across New Zealand. In a Simple, Honest and Direct way!
We pride ourselves on being a highly motivated and supportive group for people from all walks of life and fitness levels. We
encourage our members to set goals, whether it's fitness related or not, and we work hard to help you stay focused and meet them.
At Urban Athletes, our mission is to empower school leavers through a holistic approach that connects health and fitness with
essential life skills. We embrace the principles of Tuakana-Teina, fostering strong relationships where mentors guide and support
young individuals on their journey to personal and professional growth. Our goal is to create a supportive community that cultivates
strength, confidence, and resilience, enabling youth to tackle any career path they choose.
We have strategically adopted a successful partnership model with organisations to help us run different programmes to support the
community. We help underprivileged youth, mums and bubs, parents, professional athletes and local clubs. Tuakana Teina; our
careers pathway programme is designed to help young adults in Porirua have better control over their lives. This programme will
give them the resources and tools to enter the world, get a job and sustain a happy and healthy way of living.

TT wellbeing program
Physical and Mental Wellbeing
This wellbeing programme is designed to engage our students
through health and wellbeing. A strong body sets a foundation
for a strong mind.
The programme is a balance between improving physical
health as well as training mental endurance and resilience.
We set a foundation of structured rhythms and routines as well
as push physical and mental limits.
We have physical testing for strength and endurance, fitness
and also mix that in with wellbeing sessions that are more
thought provoking.
We have also spoken with professional fitness trainers and
facilities to get the best results but also engagement from the
students.
We are creating a sustainable life choice and routine for our
students to stay engaged and increase motivation for all
aspects of life including employment.
Additional Notes:
Hydration and Nutrition:
Emphasize the importance of hydration and proper nutrition
throughout the program.
Correct Form and safety:
Ensure proper form is maintained, especially during
strength testing.
Adaptability:
Be flexible and adjust workouts based on individual needs
and feedback.
Health and safety:
Form to cover any health risks or injury

The work ready programme; Tuakana-Teina has been put together to isolate key
outcomes in building motivation and resources to realistically finding a job.
Having run similar workshops, this programme is designed differently to gain
better realistic outcomes. We will walk students week by week through a full
process building collateral that they can feel confident with.
By the end they will have:
● built a value set, motivation and goals
● built a complete cv they can use to apply for jobs
● navigated job boards and recruitment agency processes
● used the cv to apply for multiple jobs
● the necessary resources to register for a recruitment company and will
have registered for at least 2 agencies
● built interview and phone screen answers from their experience
● interviewed for at least one role
● completed practical role plays or support through proper process
We are building confidence and resilience to go through a process then live
supporting them through applications concurrently.
