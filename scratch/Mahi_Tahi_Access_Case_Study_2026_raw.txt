CASE STUDY: Mahi Tahi & Access Community Health
Partnership
Demonstrating Sustainable Employment Outcomes through Structured Preparation and Holistic Support
At a Glance: Candidex partnered with Access Community Health to fill Customer Service Representative vacancies. Initially, the
recruitment opportunity was for four vacant positions. Following the group assessment centre, Access Community Health
increased their intake to seven vacancies, offering permanent, full-time employment to all seven Mahi Tahi programme candidates
who completed the process. All seven candidates commenced employment on 15th June 2026.
1. The Opportunity & Headcount Expansion
Access Community Health provides essential home support and nursing services across New Zealand. Their contact centre operations
require Customer Service Representatives capable of managing high-volume enquiries with empathy, active listening, and clear
communication. The initial vacancy headcount was expanded due to the quality of candidate performance:
Employer: Access Community Health (Level 2, Forsyth Barr Tower, Hutt Central)
Role Category: Customer Service Representative (Permanent, Full-time, 30+ hours/week)
Recruitment Process: Candidates selected through an interactive group assessment centre-style interview
2. Overcoming Employment Barriers: The Mahi Tahi Solution
Candidates referred from community programmes frequently encounter systemic and personal barriers to employment. The Mahi Tahi
programme structures practical solutions to address these barriers directly:
A. Reference Verification & Onboarding Speed
Barrier: Delays in accessing referees or completing reference audits can slow down hiring decisions, causing qualified candidates to accept other
opportunities or creating administrative backlogs.
Mahi Tahi Solution: At the client's request, reference checking was completed rapidly using Candidex's digital platform. While
background vetting requirements are typically managed case-by-case with clients preferring their own processes, Candidex has the
capability to fully support all background vetting and compliance steps to speed up onboarding.
B. Interview Anxiety & Collaborative Gaps
Barrier: Interactive group assessment centres can induce social anxiety, causing candidates to withdraw or appear overly competitive.
Mahi Tahi Solution: Weekly Waka Ama sessions built collective peer support, physical coordination, and trust. Combined with simulated
Mock Assessment Centres in Week 6, this allowed candidates to demonstrate collaborative communication during the actual interviews.
C. Professional Communication & Readiness
Barrier: Difficulty structuring work history and answering behavioral questions during formal panels.
Mahi Tahi Solution: Focused coaching on the STAR method (Week 5) and tailored company briefings prepared candidates with relevant
questions for the interview panel.

4. Delivery Insights & Key Takeaways
The outcomes achieved in this recruitment intake highlight key operational advantages for workforce recruitment and community
partnerships:
01 Reducing Employer Hiring Friction
By completing reference checks using our digital platform at the client's request, Candidex minimized employer administrative overhead.
While background vetting is typically managed case-by-case by clients, Candidex offers the capability to fully manage vetting compliance
steps to accelerate onboarding.
02 Aligning Preparation with Assessment Formats
Structuring preparation around the specific interview format (group assessment centres) ensures candidates demonstrate strong active
listening and collaborative behaviors.
03 Physical Team-building as a Soft-Skill Accelerator
The inclusion of Waka Ama acts as a practical mechanism to build and demonstrate peer support and coordination, which candidates
transfer directly to workplace team structures.
