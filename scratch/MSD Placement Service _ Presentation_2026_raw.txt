MSD Employment Placement Service
Service Delivery Model & Transition Framework (Through June 2027)
This service operates as a direct extension to support work-ready candidates referred by MSD who have completed another provider's programme
but were not placed. We ingest previous Exit Reports to analyse candidate-specific gaps, deploy targeted 4-week module training tracks to address
barriers, and actively match graduates directly into live employer vacancies.
